## The commission

The creative subject is the bank's whole collapse, not one joint, one material, or a list of defects. Give it the concentrated authorship and effort that the original broad prompt gave the entire district. The existing architecture, inhabited court, connected pieces, effects, and rewind are your starting advantage. Use them to create a more compelling event, not merely a slightly improved implementation of the same event.

The promise to the player is:

> **Where I cut changes how the building comes down. I can follow recognizable construction losing support, moving with weight and direction, and changing what happens next. Rewinding and choosing a different cut is worth doing because it creates a meaningfully different event.**

This is not a request to turn up horizontal velocity, make every charge a stronger explosion, or add a “fall left/right” control. Outward motion is one possibility, not the mandated answer. Inward movement, uneven descent, and a relatively compact collapse may also be appropriate. Direction should arise from the changed construction, remaining supports, connections, forces, and contact—not the camera, a scenario identifier, or an authored end pose.

You choose the coherent design that makes this experience exceptional. Intelligible cause and effect need not mean perfect player prediction, exact left/right symmetry, or a complete civil-engineering simulation.
