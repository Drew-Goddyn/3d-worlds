# First media-only review

A fresh context received only baseline screenshots, recording/frame strip, neutral action/time labels and the supplied visual-director brief. It did not receive implementation, test results or builder explanations. The reviewer inspected stills and expressly declined motion claims. The initial study used a direct swing from the crane's high starting position, before the later matched street approach was established.

> The bank exterior already reads convincingly; the banking hall does not yet become the reward for breaching it.
>
> Preserve the paired columns, tall arched glazing and restrained bank sign. Cream masonry against teal glass and red neighbors suits the bright stylized world.
>
> In the breached view, the entrance and almost the entire ground floor disappear behind pale rubble. The other angle also reveals mostly debris. Damage is unmistakable, but banking activity and contents are not readable; this does not prove they are absent.
>
> Highest-value emphasis: a recognizable teller area with contrasting furnishings and small contents that remain legible among masonry. This is a proposed experience. Stills cannot establish material motion or satisfying timing.

Builder decision: author one connected teller hall, preserve the warm civic exterior and local support failures, and make reachable furnishings contact-driven. The final paired recordings use the same native crane setup on both versions so the ball approaches the ground-floor windows from the street. This review is advice, not owner acceptance.
